Appendix 1 - Research & Development, Sampling and Formulation Terms

Where a product requires formulation development, flavour development, sample preparation, reformulation, technical review, dossier preparation, or manufacturing validation, the following Research & Development, Sampling and Formulation Terms shall apply unless otherwise agreed in writing by Vita Manufacture Limited.

These terms are intended to ensure that the customer, Sales team, R&D team, Production team, and Quality team are fully aligned before development, or manufacturing work begins.

1. Purpose of the R&D Phase

The purpose of the R&D phase is to develop a commercially viable product formulation that can be manufactured within Vita Manufacture's production capabilities.

R&D work may include, where applicable:

  • formulation development;
  • formulation adjustment;
  • flavour development;
  • taste, texture, colour, aroma, and mouthfeel optimisation;
  • ingredient compatibility review;
  • technical feasibility review;
  • manufacturing feasibility review;
  • sample preparation;
  • review of active ingredient levels;
  • review of dosage format and serving size;
  • draft Product Specification Sheet preparation;
  • basic label guidance based on the agreed formulation;
  • product dossier preparation, where included in the agreed package.

R&D is a development process and does not guarantee that every requested taste, texture, colour, cost target, active level, claim, ingredient combination, or dosage format will be achievable.

Some formulations may require compromise due to regulatory, technical, stability, solubility, palatability, manufacturing, raw material, capsule size, powder density, cost, or commercial limitations.

2. R&D Packages

Unless otherwise stated in the quotation, Vita Manufacture offers the following R&D options.

A. Complimentary R&D with Confirmed Production Order

Complimentary R&D may be offered where the customer places a confirmed production order and pays the required deposit.

Complimentary R&D includes:

  • formulation support for one agreed product concept;
  • up to two R&D iterations per product;
  • a maximum of two flavour options per product, where flavour development is relevant;
  • sample preparation within the agreed product brief;
  • reasonable technical input required to finalise the Product Specification Sheet for production.

Complimentary R&D is provided on a goodwill and production-linked basis. It is only available where the customer proceeds with a confirmed production order.

If the customer cancels, delays, materially changes, or does not proceed to production after R&D work has started, Vita Manufacture reserves the right to charge for R&D work already completed, including sample preparation, technical time, raw materials, courier costs, supplier costs, and administrative costs.

Additional samples, reformulations, flavour options, or iterations outside the complimentary allowance shall be charged at £250 + VAT per additional R&D sample or iteration per product, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

B. Full R&D Package

Where the customer purchases Vita Manufacture's Full R&D Package, the fee shall be £1,750 + VAT per product, unless otherwise stated in the quotation.

The Full R&D Package is suitable for customers who require structured product development before committing to production.

The Full R&D Package includes:

  • formulation development for one product concept;
  • up to seven R&D iterations;
  • sample preparation within the agreed development scope;
  • technical formulation review;
  • ingredient compatibility review;
  • manufacturing feasibility review;
  • product dossier preparation;
  • draft Product Specification Sheet;
  • basic label guidance based on the agreed formulation;
  • expert product listing on Vita Manufacture's website, where applicable and approved by Vita Manufacture.

For clarity, one product means one product type, one dosage format, one primary formula direction, and one primary product concept.

Different dosage formats, substantially different ingredient profiles, different flavour families, different target markets, or separate SKUs may be treated as separate products and may require separate R&D fees.

Additional samples, reformulations, flavour options, or iterations outside the Full R&D Package shall be charged at £250 + VAT per additional R&D sample or iteration per product, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

C. Diamond R&D Package

Where the customer requires a more flexible development process, Vita Manufacture may offer the Diamond R&D Package at £3,500 + VAT per product, unless otherwise stated in the quotation.

The Diamond R&D Package is designed for premium, complex, investor-ready, flavour-sensitive, or highly customised products where additional development flexibility may be required before production.

The Diamond R&D Package includes:

  • formulation development for one product concept;
  • reasonable sample support within the agreed project scope;
  • up to twelve R&D iterations;
  • up to four flavour options per product, where flavour development is relevant;
  • technical formulation review;
  • ingredient compatibility review;
  • manufacturing feasibility review;
  • sample preparation within the agreed development scope;
  • product dossier preparation;
  • material specification sheet;
  • basic label guidance based on the agreed formulation;
  • Vita Manufacture expert list on client website where applicable and approved by Vita Manufacture;
  • priority R&D scheduling where capacity allows.

For clarity, "reasonable sample support" means a commercially reasonable level of sampling and development support within the agreed product concept, formula direction, dosage format, target market, and product brief.

Reasonable sample support does not mean unlimited reformulation, unlimited flavour families, unlimited ingredient changes, unlimited dosage format changes, unlimited courier dispatches, unlimited product concepts, or unlimited SKUs.

Additional samples, reformulations, flavour options, or iterations outside the Diamond R&D Package may be charged at £250 + VAT per additional R&D sample or iteration per product, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

4. Additional Samples and Extra Iterations

Any R&D sample, reformulation, flavour option, technical adjustment, or iteration outside the included allowance shall be charged at £250 + VAT per additional R&D sample or iteration per product, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

  • Additional charges may apply where the customer requests:
  • further flavour trials beyond the included allowance;
  • changes to the active ingredient profile;
  • changes to the dosage format;
  • changes to the serving size;
  • replacement of key ingredients;
  • reformulation due to customer preference rather than technical failure;
  • reformulation due to new claims, target markets, or regulatory requirements;
  • repeat samples due to delayed, unclear, or inconsistent feedback;
  • samples requiring specialist, premium, rare, custom, or high-cost raw materials;
  • courier or international delivery of samples;
  • third-party laboratory analysis;
  • full stability testing;
  • specialist regulatory submissions;
  • additional dossier work outside the agreed scope.

Vita Manufacture may pause R&D work until any additional R&D fees, sample charges, raw material charges, or courier charges are paid.

5. Customer Feedback and Approval

The customer must provide clear, consolidated feedback after each sample round.

Feedback should be specific and commercially actionable, including comments on taste, sweetness, texture, colour, aroma, mouthfeel, dosage format, serving size, and any desired changes.

Where feedback is unclear, contradictory, subjective, or provided in stages by multiple people, Vita Manufacture may request clarification before continuing development.

Where additional samples are required because of unclear, delayed, contradictory, or changing customer feedback, the next sample may be treated as an additional iteration and may be chargeable.

The customer is responsible for ensuring that all relevant internal stakeholders review the sample before feedback is submitted.

Once Vita Manufacture proceeds based on customer feedback, any later change of direction may be treated as a new iteration and may be chargeable.

6. Meaning of "Reasonable Sample Support"

Where reasonable sample support is included, it means Vita Manufacture will provide a commercially reasonable level of sample development support within the agreed product brief.

Reasonable sample support is limited by:

  • the agreed product concept;
  • the agreed dosage format;
  • the agreed formula direction;
  • the agreed target market;
  • the agreed flavour direction;
  • the agreed commercial brief;
  • technical feasibility;
  • manufacturing feasibility;
  • raw material availability;
  • R&D capacity;
  • regulatory suitability;
  • cost parameters.

Reasonable sample support does not include:

  • unlimited reformulation;
  • unlimited flavour development;
  • unlimited flavour families;
  • unlimited ingredient changes;
  • unlimited dosage format changes;
  • unlimited active level changes;
  • unlimited courier dispatches;
  • unlimited third-party testing;
  • unlimited product concepts;
  • unlimited SKUs;
  • repeated changes in customer direction;
  • work outside the agreed product brief.

Any sample support outside the agreed scope may be quoted separately or charged at the additional R&D sample rate.

7. Material Formula Changes

A material formula change may be treated as a new R&D project and may require a new quotation.

Material formula changes include, but are not limited to:

  • changing the dosage format, for example from capsules to powder, gummies, liquids, tablets, sachets, or softgels;
  • changing the main active ingredients;
  • changing the intended product function;
  • changing the product's target market;
  • changing the regulatory market or sales territory;
  • changing from one flavour family to another after development has started;
  • changing the product from standard to vegan, allergen-free, organic, halal, sugar-free, natural-only, clean-label, or similar positioning where this affects formulation;
  • changing the required claims or active ingredient levels;
  • changing the target cost after ingredients have been sourced or samples prepared;
  • creating additional SKUs, variants, strengths, pack sizes, or flavour families.

8. Technical, Regulatory and Manufacturing Limitations

All formulations are subject to technical, regulatory, quality, manufacturing, and raw material feasibility checks.

Vita Manufacture may refuse, amend, or recommend alternatives to any ingredient, claim, dosage, flavour, colour, capsule size, serving size, excipient, sweetener, dosage format, or product format where Vita Manufacture considers the request unsuitable, impractical, non-compliant, unstable, unsafe, commercially unrealistic, or incompatible with its manufacturing processes.

The customer acknowledges that some ingredients may not be suitable for the requested dosage format, serving size, flavour profile, target market, claim, or manufacturing process.

The customer remains responsible for confirming that the final product, formulation, label, claims, presentation, marketing, advertising, and sale are suitable and lawful for the customer's intended sales channels, customer base, and sales territories.

9. R&D Timelines

Any R&D timelines provided are estimates only.

R&D timelines may vary depending on:

  • complexity of the formulation;
  • number of products;
  • dosage format;
  • flavour complexity;
  • raw material availability;
  • supplier lead times;
  • flavour house lead times;
  • speed and clarity of customer feedback;
  • number of iterations required;
  • regulatory review requirements;
  • courier timelines;
  • internal R&D capacity;
  • whether additional samples or reformulations are required.

Manufacturing lead times shall not begin until:

  • the final Product Specification Sheet has been approved and signed;
  • label requirements have been satisfied;
  • required payments have been received;
  • production-critical materials have been confirmed;
  • all production-critical matters have been finalised.

10. Sample Approval and Production Scale-Up

Samples are prepared for evaluation purposes only and may not be sold, distributed, marketed, supplied to third parties, or used for consumer testing unless Vita Manufacture gives written approval.

Sample approval by the customer confirms approval of the product's general taste, appearance, format, and development direction.

The customer acknowledges that small-scale R&D samples and full-scale production batches may not be identical.

Minor differences may occur in:

  • taste;
  • colour;
  • texture;
  • aroma;
  • density;
  • solubility;
  • mouthfeel;
  • fill weight;
  • capsule appearance;
  • powder flow;
  • finished product appearance.

Such differences may occur due to scale-up, raw material variation, manufacturing tolerances, processing conditions, and natural ingredient variation.

These differences shall not constitute a defect where the product remains within agreed specification and normal manufacturing tolerances.

11. Ownership of Formulations and R&D Work

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, all technical know-how, manufacturing methods, supplier knowledge, production processes, process knowledge, and formulation development work created by Vita Manufacture remain the intellectual property and confidential know-how of Vita Manufacture.

Where a customer provides its own pre-existing formulation, the customer remains responsible for its ownership, suitability, legality, safety, and right to use that formulation.

Where Vita Manufacture develops a formulation for the customer, the customer receives the right to purchase finished goods manufactured by Vita Manufacture using the approved formulation.

The customer does not automatically receive ownership of Vita Manufacture's manufacturing know-how, supplier information, technical process methods, internal R&D records, or confidential development documentation unless this is separately agreed in writing.

If the customer cancels the project, delays the project, fails to provide feedback, fails to pay the required deposit or balance, materially changes the project, or chooses not to proceed to production after R&D work has started, Vita Manufacture reserves the right to charge for work and costs already incurred.

  • This may include:
  • R&D time;
  • sample preparation;
  • raw materials;
  • supplier costs;
  • courier costs;
  • technical review;
  • administrative work;
  • formulation work;
  • dossier work;
  • third-party costs;
  • any other project-specific costs incurred.

Where complimentary R&D was offered on the basis of a confirmed production order, but the customer does not proceed to production, Vita Manufacture may charge the customer the standard R&D rate or a reasonable proportion of the R&D work completed.

13. Sales Representations and Written Agreement

Only the R&D scope, sample allowance, pricing, timelines, and deliverables stated in the written quotation, signed proposal, invoice, Product Specification Sheet, or written agreement shall be binding.

No verbal statement, sales discussion, call note, meeting note, email comment, WhatsApp message, or informal assurance shall amend the R&D scope unless confirmed in writing by an authorised representative of Vita Manufacture.

Where there is any conflict between a verbal discussion and the written proposal, the written proposal shall prevail.

This clause is intended to ensure that customer expectations, Sales commitments, R&D deliverables, Production requirements, and Quality obligations remain aligned before work begins.
